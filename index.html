A little about me

The internship ended. The questions stayed with me.

At Southwire, I saw how much time recurring data work could take away from operational review. After my internship ended in September, I kept learning on my own, exploring automation and AI tools and building around the problem that stayed with me. I’m interested in how thoughtful systems can give people more time to focus on operations.
