A little about me

The internship ended. My curiosity didn’t.

At Southwire, I saw how hours of morning data work could delay the team's view of unusual orders. After my internship ended in September, I kept learning on my own, exploring automation and AI tools, and building around that operational challenge. This portfolio brings together that work and other questions I’ve been curious enough to explore.

“I like understanding the work behind the numbers, then finding a clearer way for people to use them.”David Nguyen · Business analytics and supply chain
How I work

Start with people. Make the signal useful.

Across different industries and questions, I return to the same habits: get close to the real problem, explain the insight in plain language, and keep people involved in the decision.

01

Understand the routine

Learn where the work slows down and what people need to decide with confidence.

02

Make the complex clear

Turn data and analysis into a view people can understand without a manual.

03

Keep learning by building

Explore tools, test ideas, and improve the experience one useful step at a time.
